About the Hall ♡

about this page...

The name “AHOF,” short for “All-time Hall Of Famer,” was created by F&F Entertainment to represent their vision of the group becoming lasting and influential figures in the K-pop industry—just like the legendary names remembered in a “hall of fame.” ✨

There’s also a fun meaning behind the name! In Korean, “AHOF” sounds similar to the word for the number 9, which represents the group’s nine members. It also reflects the idea that their journey is still continuing, with plenty of room to grow, create memories, and make their mark together. 💙
